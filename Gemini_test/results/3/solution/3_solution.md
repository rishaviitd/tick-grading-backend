# Solution for 3

**Question:** 3. Answer any one of the following questions:

**Question Type:** Subjective

---

**Reasoning:**
- This question has no sub-parts. It is worth 5 marks, requiring [5 to 10] steps for the entire solution. **I will strictly adhere to this step count for each method.**

**Method solution:**
- **Method 1: Following the Instruction to Select and Answer**
    - The student must first understand the core instruction to choose only one question from the provided set.
    - They need to examine the available options presented to understand their content.
    - A deliberate selection must be made between the options based on the instruction.
    - The chosen option must be clearly indicated on the answer script.
    - The student must then commence answering the selected question to demonstrate adherence to the instruction.

---
**Method 1: Step-by-step adherence to instruction**
---
- Step 1:
  - Step ID: 1
  - Teacher Expectation: Reads and comprehends the primary instruction to select only one question from the provided options.
  - Pass if: Demonstrates understanding that only one question from the set is to be answered.
  - Fail if: Attempts to answer all questions or none of the questions.
  - Mark Type: B
- Step 2:
  - Step ID: 2
  - Teacher Expectation: Identifies and acknowledges the presence of distinct questions offered as choices.
  - Pass if: Recognizes that there are multiple options available to answer.
  - Fail if: Ignores the existence of the options or treats them as a single entity.
  - Mark Type: M
- Step 3:
  - Step ID: 3
  - Teacher Expectation: Examines the first presented option (e.g., Option A) to understand its content.
  - Pass if: Shows evidence of having processed the content of at least one option.
  - Fail if: Completely bypasses the examination of any option without making a choice.
  - Mark Type: M
- Step 4:
  - Step ID: 4
  - Teacher Expectation: Examines the second presented option (e.g., Option B) to understand its content.
  - Pass if: Shows evidence of having processed the content of the second option.
  - Fail if: Fails to process the content of the second option and makes an arbitrary choice without basis.
  - Mark Type: M
- Step 5:
  - Step ID: 5
  - Teacher Expectation: Makes a clear and definitive selection of one question to answer.
  - Pass if: A single, unambiguous choice of question is made.
  - Fail if: The choice is ambiguous, multiple options are selected, or no choice is made.
  - Mark Type: A
- Step 6:
  - Step ID: 6
  - Teacher Expectation: Clearly indicates which selected question is being answered.
  - Pass if: The chosen question is explicitly stated (e.g., "Answer to Question 3(a):" or "Option B:").
  - Fail if: The indication of the chosen question is missing or unclear.
  - Mark Type: A
- Step 7:
  - Step ID: 7
  - Teacher Expectation: Commences the answer for the selected question, demonstrating commitment to the choice.
  - Pass if: The student begins to write the response to the chosen question.
  - Fail if: The student indicates a choice but does not begin answering it.
  - Mark Type: A
