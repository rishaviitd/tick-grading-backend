# Solution for 23_secondary

**Question:** (b) Two numbers are in the ratio 4:5 and their HCF is 11. Find the LCM of these numbers.

**Question Type:** Subjective

---

**Reasoning:**
- This question has no sub-parts. It is worth 2 marks, requiring [2 to 4] steps for the entire solution.
- **I will strictly adhere to this step count for each method.**
- Bloom's Level: Applying

**Method solution:**
- **Method 1:** The plan is to represent the two numbers using their ratio and HCF, calculate the actual numbers, use the property `Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM`, and then solve for the LCM.
- **Method 2:** The plan is to state the property that if two numbers are in the ratio `a:b` and their HCF is `h`, then their LCM is `abh`, and apply this formula with the given values.

---
**Method 1: Using the Product-HCF-LCM Relationship**
---
- Step 1:
  - Step ID: 1
  - Teacher Expectation: Represents the two numbers in the ratio 4:5 as `4x` and `5x`, and identifies that since their HCF is 11, `x` must be 11.
  - Pass if: Correctly represents the numbers as `4x` and `5x` and correctly deduces `x = 11`.
  - Fail if: Incorrect representation of numbers or incorrect identification of `x`.
  - Mark Type: B
- Step 2:
  - Step ID: 2
  - Teacher Expectation: Calculates the actual values of the two numbers by substituting `x = 11`.
  - Pass if: Correctly calculates the numbers as `4 × 11 = 44` and `5 × 11 = 55`.
  - Fail if: Calculation error in finding either number.
  - Mark Type: M
- Step 3:
  - Step ID: 3
  - Teacher Expectation: States the fundamental relationship between the product of two numbers, their HCF, and their LCM.
  - Pass if: Correctly states `Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM`.
  - Fail if: States an incorrect relationship.
  - Mark Type: B
- Step 4:
  - Step ID: 4
  - Teacher Expectation: Substitutes the calculated numbers and the given HCF into the relationship and solves for the LCM.
  - Pass if: Correctly calculates `LCM = (44 × 55) / 11 = 220`.
  - Fail if: Incorrect substitution or calculation error leading to the wrong LCM.
  - Mark Type: A

---
**Method 2: Using the LCM property for numbers in ratio**
---
- Step 1:
  - Step ID: 1
  - Teacher Expectation: States the property that if two numbers are in the ratio `a:b` and their HCF is `h`, then their LCM is `abh`.
  - Pass if: Correctly states or implies the property `LCM = a × b × h` for numbers in ratio `a:b` with HCF `h`.
  - Fail if: States an incorrect property or formula.
  - Mark Type: B
- Step 2:
  - Step ID: 2
  - Teacher Expectation: Identifies the values of `a`, `b`, and `h` from the given problem statement.
  - Pass if: Correctly identifies `a=4`, `b=5`, and `h=11`.
  - Fail if: Incorrectly identifies any of these values.
  - Mark Type: M
- Step 3:
  - Step ID: 3
  - Teacher Expectation: Substitutes these identified values into the LCM formula `LCM = a × b × h`.
  - Pass if: Correctly substitutes the values to get `LCM = 4 × 5 × 11`.
  - Fail if: Incorrect substitution into the formula.
  - Mark Type: M
- Step 4:
  - Step ID: 4
  - Teacher Expectation: Calculates the final LCM from the substituted expression.
  - Pass if: Correctly calculates `4 × 5 × 11 = 220`.
  - Fail if: Calculation error leads to the wrong LCM.
  - Mark Type: A
