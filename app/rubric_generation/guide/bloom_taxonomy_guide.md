# Bloom's Taxonomy Guide

## 1. Remembering

**What it assesses:** The ability to retrieve facts, definitions, and basic concepts directly from memory.

**Trigger Verbs / Keywords:** List, Name, Define, Identify, Select, Label, State, Recall.

## 2. Understanding

**What it assesses:** The ability to construct meaning from information and explain concepts in one's own words.

**Trigger Verbs / Keywords:** Explain, Describe, Discuss, Summarize, Give an example, Differentiate, Interpret, Paraphrase.

## 3. Applying

**What it assesses:** The ability to use learned knowledge and procedures in a new, concrete situation or to solve a problem.

**Trigger Verbs / Keywords:** Use, Solve, Calculate, Demonstrate, Apply, Implement, Show, Draw (a diagram to solve).

## 4. Analyzing

**What it assesses:** The ability to break down information into its component parts and determine how these parts relate to each other and to an overall structure.

**Trigger Verbs / Keywords:** Analyze, Compare, Contrast, Connect, Deconstruct, Examine, Find the relationship, Hence.

## 5. Evaluating

**What it assesses:** The ability to make judgments and justify decisions based on specific criteria, standards, and evidence-based reasoning.

**Trigger Verbs / Keywords:** Justify, Critique, Defend, Rate, Assess, Argue, "Do you agree?", "Which is better?".

## 6. Creating

**What it assesses:** The ability to put various elements together to generate a new, coherent product or point of view. This includes designing experiments, formulating plans, or inventing models.

**Trigger Verbs / Keywords:** Design, Formulate, Propose, Create, Invent, Develop a plan/model.
